HOW TO PLAY DUST
  1. 1
    MOVE THE NEEDLE (OUTER ↔ INNER). Drag the tonearm radially. Outer wax is lush Detroit jazz-house (vibraphone, Wurlitzer, upright bass); inner wax strips down into hypnotic Jelinek micro-loops. Tap the spindle or dock the arm to stop/start.
  2. 2
    TAP & SMUDGE THE WAX. Tap anywhere on the vinyl for a crisp dust speck (modal ping, brushed tick, or skipping-needle repeat). Hold or smear for a warm granular smudge cloud. Double-tap a mark to flick it off. The glowing focus band follows your needle.
  3. 3
    RIDE THE STROBE RIM / LABEL. Drag the outer metal strobe rim or center white label to pitch-bend timing, hold to brake the motor, or back-spin to scrub a fresh groove variation. Double-tap the white label to flip Side A/B.
