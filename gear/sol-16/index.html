HOW TO JAM
  1. 1
    PRESS PLAY (Space) — switch patterns A–H on top or build an automatic Song Chain in ≡ Menu.
  2. 2
    TWIST THE 4 KNOBS — SND shapes TONE, DECAY, SPACE & DRIVE; MIX dials granular VOL, PAN, DUST & M.VOL; MSTR controls BPM, SWING, PUMP & COMP.
  3. 3
    GRID OR PIANO KEYS — paint chords/drums on the 4×4 grid, or tap KEYS (or P) for a chromatic multi-touch piano with scale dots & custom polyphonic chord recording!
  4. 4
    EXPORT ANYWHERE — save complete songs + custom samples as .sol16.json, export multi-track .MID for Ableton/Logic, or record .WAV.
