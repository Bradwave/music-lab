HOW TO PLAY 3AM
  1. 1
    MINT = YOUR ANCHORS. Tap anywhere on a tape to place or remove solid hits. Drag an anchor up/down to change its note, chord, or drum.
  2. 2
    AMBER = MICRO DUST. Twist MICRO to weave subtle micro-clicks, shuffles, and dub whispers around your anchors without touching them.
  3. 3
    POLYRHYTHMS & PHASE. Drag the numbered right tape roller (3–16) to stretch/shorten loops, drag empty tape left/right to slip phase, and drag the HEAD bar to sculpt tone.
  4. 4
    LIVE DUB & TAP. Hold SUB / CUT / CHD / AIR (or A S D F) for live dub-echo throws, or arm TAP to punch your own groove live.
